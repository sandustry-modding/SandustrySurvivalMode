# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Added: health on the resource HUD (`current/100`).
- Added: **Fire**, **flame**, and **lava** damage while overlapping (lava hits harder). The player sprite tints orange or red.
- Added: **Jump** on Boost (Up / W) or **Space**.
- Added: hold **Sprint** on Shift while moving on ground. Vanilla Sprint Boost burst is off.
- Added: stronger gravity and **fall damage** after drops longer than 28 cells.
- Added: auto step-up over ledges (default 3 cells). Hover flight is off.
- Added: **H** restores health to 100 (debug). Rebind under Options → Controls.
- Added: **Mod enabled** and **Max step cells** in Options → Mods.
